OpenSTT turns your voice into text, notes, and actions. Press a hotkey, speak, and your words appear at your cursor. Choose local speech-to-text models for offline transcription or connect a cloud provider.

## Features

- **Voice dictation** — dictate into any app with a global hotkey and automatic pasting.
- **Translation** — speak in one language and paste text in another.
- **AI assistant** — use voice commands to ask questions, edit text, and get help.
- **Meeting transcription** — record and transcribe conversations with speaker identification.
- **Notes** — organize and search your notes, then use AI to work with them.
- **Audio import** — transcribe existing audio and video files.
- **Local or cloud processing** — choose how your transcriptions are processed.
